Limits
------
Transcript support applies to the assigned passages. Automatic captions can
contain errors; per-source notes state exclusions and corroboration. A few
publisher sources supply narration scripts rather than spoken transcripts.
This audit does not certify every statement in full recordings or external
books, successful provider playback everywhere, clinical competence, graduate
mastery or an accredited qualification. Required readings, native exercises and
independent assessments remain part of each lesson. No full third-party
transcripts are distributed in the site or this report.

Verification results are recorded in the delivery report after the production
build and browser checks; audit completion alone is not deployment evidence.
