Limits
--------
Editorial coverage cannot guarantee every beginner will find every explanation
clear. Full university prerequisites and specialist clinical training remain
required where stated. No learner outcomes, clinical competence, external exam
readiness or research novelty are established. A qualified subject specialist
and real learner feedback can reveal further improvements.

No live model inference, transcription, token-count probe, replay, scoring matrix,
child assessment, clinical trial, recording upload or new provider evaluation was
run. Earlier video audits retain their exact scopes; this pass did not reverify
all unassigned speech or every assertion in external textbooks.
