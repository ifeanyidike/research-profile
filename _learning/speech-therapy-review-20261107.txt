Speech Therapy Studio practical release — 7 November 2026

Scope
General parent education and low-pressure everyday communication support for
preschool children using few words. No diagnosis, treatment prescription,
qualification or guaranteed outcome. No child data is collected by the new
activities or paper observation sheet. Existing browser-local adult learning
schema and S01–S20 identifiers are preserved.

Coverage addressed
- S07: choosing useful word meanings, natural repetition across situations,
  and responding to gestures without withholding help.
- S08: expansions, grammatical adult models, person/action, action/object,
  possession, description and location. Gesture-plus-word examples and limits
  of conclusions drawn from a memorised phrase.
- S09: worked in/out example, context support, short messages and refusal.
- S12/S15: optional simple pretend play and voluntary action/sound copying;
  neither a diagnostic task nor a speech-sound drill.
- S19: distinguish adult model, immediate copying, independent use and a
  different situation, with actual support conditions; no word quota.
- S20 and choosing guide: repeat, simplify, vary one thing, end and seek
  individual advice when needed. No algorithm selecting treatment.
- 12 original activity cards linked from every lesson. Each contains setup,
  example adult language, an invented exchange, ordered steps, responses to
  gestures/unclear words/silence/refusal/frustration, easier option, ending,
  a possible later variation and scoped source links.
- Private printable observation sheet with fictional filled examples and
  space for handwritten notes. No app form or child record, no daily trials.
- Existing three-item navigation retained. Activities and choosing guide
  highlight Lessons. Activity routes do not change adult lesson history.
- Home recommendation now correctly says Continue after a later lesson was
  opened, even when the adult has not checked off any lesson.

Sources checked
ASHA Semantic Relationships:
https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/late-language-emergence/semantic-relationships/
Used to check the variety of meaning relationships. Child examples are not
adult scripts; our models retain natural grammar. No required learning order.

The Hanen Centre, How to Help Your Child Learn New Words:
https://www.hanen.org/information-tips/how-to-help-your-child-learn-new-words
Practical interests/gestures/natural repetition sections checked. Age tables
and repetition counts are not imported as a home score or prescribed dose.

Speech and Language UK, Add words to what your child says:
https://speechandlanguage.org.uk/help-for-families/resource-library-for-families/everyday-tips-for-supporting-your-child/supporting-communication-skills-in-the-early-years/add-words-to-what-your-child-says/
Read repeat/add, no correction demands and no required repetition. Source
acknowledges different developmental routes. Written source assigned to S08.

Newcastle Hospitals NHS: following the child's lead, comments and supporting
understanding; scoped source URLs are in lessons.json. Original activity
examples apply general strategies rather than reproduce publisher scripts.
