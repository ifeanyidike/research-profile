Speech Therapy Studio — parent education
=======================================
Research/authoring date: 6 November 2026
Scope: preschool communication support, especially few words and emerging word
combinations. Twenty original parent lessons, not a professional qualification,
diagnostic tool, personalised treatment plan or substitute for assessment.

Canonical content: speech-therapy-studio/lessons.json. Every lesson has a defined
purpose, prerequisite links, three explanatory paragraphs, a fictional worked
conversation, materials, ordered activities, adaptations, limits, an adult
rehearsal, a learning question and exact free primary-source assignments.
Sources: ASHA/CHSA, NIDCD, NHS services, Hanen, Harvard and CDC. Do not require a paid
guidebook or programme. Do not publish copied third-party transcripts.

Short optional video assignments retain exact clocks from the prior scoped
transcript audit: Harvard serve/return 6:06; NHS social-communication support
6:28; NHS choice-board 0:00–6:29 of 7:27. The named NHS publisher transcripts
were read for relevance and boundaries. The Harvard original guide was checked;
the exact recording/caption evidence is inherited from the previous audit.
The choice-board tail instruction about accidental selections is excluded.
External links may continue past the stated stop; the studio says so explicitly.
Other units on a collection page, including object-exchange physical prompting,
are not assigned. Broad-age examples do not define requirements for age three.

Content checks: separate speech/language/communication; everyday response does
not rule out partial/fluctuating hearing loss; invite assessment in parallel;
define unfamiliar terms; model meaningful grammatical language; accept gestures,
AAC and refusal; never withhold essentials, force eye contact, physically guide
selections, require imitation, prescribe mouth exercises, diagnose or guarantee
words by a date. Feeding difficulties are not treated by these lessons.
Private family circumstances are not included in the published material.

Product: three navigation items, readable single-column lessons, optional
reference/rehearsal details, previous/next navigation, visible quiet/difficult
adaptations, separate local adult checkmarks and validated backup merge.
No child name, recording, clinical note or free-text health-data form. No sign-in
or hosted sync is claimed. Existing account/progress schemas are untouched.

Validation limits: source review and offline checks do not establish clinical
effectiveness, comprehension by a human beginner, or professional review of an
individual child's needs. No child interaction or clinical treatment trial is
performed by the authoring tools. No model-provider runs are permitted.
