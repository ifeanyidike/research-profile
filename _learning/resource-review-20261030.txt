Scope and method
177 topics: Cyber 93, Voice AI 44, Child Development 40.
Baseline: 734 assignments, 566 distinct public URLs. Every baseline URL was
requested. Publisher pages, titles, uploader identities, available descriptions,
chapter boundaries and transcripts were compared with lesson assignments. 36 PDF
and 11 slide decks were downloaded/extracted to check their identities and topic
coverage. A successful page request is not evidence of full video playback.
Final catalogue: 304 video assignments, 410 readings and 39 practical links.
123 explicitly bounded YouTube excerpts, plus named clips/sections on provider
pages. Whole lectures remain where their connected argument is required.

Implementation and learner records
The shared renderer shows assignments, optional status, full running times when
verified, exact start/stop points and source access notes. YouTube start/end are
absolute seconds. Embedded clips preserve both; external YouTube links seek to
the start and tell the learner where to stop. Single clips do not inherit a course
playlist. Multi-clip publisher pages are not misrepresented by their first iframe.
Players load only after a click, receive keyboard focus and meet the 200px minimum
height on narrow screens. Source-map downloads retain the assignments.
Replacement resources receive unused progress identifiers. Moving an existing
resource preserves its original identifier; old completions cannot falsely check
new material. No progress schema, account, RLS or callback change is needed.

Verification and boundaries
38 offline tests cover URL bounds, escaped rendering, prerequisite packaging,
backup/account behaviour and actual store-to-card completion identity. Mutations
that discard end bounds or substitute array-index keys fail the relevant tests.
Browser resource-page sweep checks all 177 topics against the authored catalogue.
Production Jekyll and repository-wide formatting checks are required for release.

Initial HTTP checks: 523 responses were 200, 29 were 406, 13 were 403 and one
request timed out. Some 200 pages are access challenges; these are not treated as
content verification. Six initially incomplete slide downloads were retried once;
all 47 document identities became readable. Provider blocks, account restrictions,
full playback and caption accuracy remain distinct from source alignment.
This review did not watch every complete external recording, validate every claim
in whole external textbooks, assess clinical treatment suitability or establish
graduate competence. No CAPTCHA bypass, provider inference, replay/evaluation,
training run, live lab execution, real child data or synthetic signup email was
used. Clinical cases remain educational and fictional. Fresh OAuth, email recovery
and actual two-device sync are outside this resource-renderer change.
