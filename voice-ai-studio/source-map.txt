V01 — What a voice system actually does
Prerequisites: None
Path: All learners
Study assignment: Watch the two short W3C input/output demonstrations, read the HF course introduction and Stanford scope, then map capture, ASR, dialogue, permissioned actions, TTS and transport. These demonstrations illustrate components, not a complete agent.
Video coverage/access: Two short, beginner-accessible demonstrations replace the former advanced feature-engineering clip. Speech recognition means recognising words here; speaker recognition is a different task.
